## One-Sentence Explanation

SHIN-CHAN helps a cyber-cell analyst find groups of accounts that act together online, understand whether the activity may represent a threat, preserve supporting evidence, and prepare a reviewable escalation brief.

## Important Positioning

This is a decision-support prototype: batch uploads plus a demonstration stream API, no live platform connector. It does not prove that an account is a bot, decide that a crime occurred, predict violence with certainty, or replace an investigator, supervisor, or legal officer.
